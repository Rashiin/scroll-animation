03 — Key features

Engineered for every climate, designed for living

Every layer of the Space Cabin was chosen for a reason: longer life, lower energy bills and more comfort.

01

Galvanised steel frame

Hot-dip galvanised chassis resists rust and humidity, and sits on adjustable legs.

02

Fibreglass shell

A seamless moulded body with a glossy gel-coat: light, UV-resistant and never needs painting.

03

Polyurethane insulation

Up to 100 mm of sprayed PU foam keeps summer heat and winter cold outside.

10 — FAQ

Everything you need to know

How much does the Space Cabin cost?

It depends on the version, your customisations and the installation site. You can see base prices in the comparison section, and a consultation will give you the final figure.

How long do production and delivery take?

Production takes 30–45 days depending on the version. On-site installation takes a single day.

Does it need a concrete foundation?

No. The cabin stands on adjustable galvanised legs. A levelled, compacted surface or a few small concrete pads is enough.

How does it perform in extreme heat or cold?

Up to 100 mm of PU insulation and Low-E double glazing keep it comfortable in cold mountain climates and hot southern ones alike.

Do I need a permit?

The cabin is a relocatable structure, but local rules vary. Our team will guide you during the consultation.

What can the smart system do?

It controls lighting, climate, motorised blinds and the digital lock. Zenith adds cameras and an alarm. Everything works from the mobile app, even remotely.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Payments are staged with production progress, and instalment plans are available on selected versions.

What about warranty and after-sales service?

The structure is covered for up to 10 years and equipment for 2 years, with after-sales service nationwide.
